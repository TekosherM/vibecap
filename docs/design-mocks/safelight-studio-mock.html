How heavy should your captures be?

This sets recording resolution, the retro buffer, and how long media is kept. You can change it anytime in Settings → Budget.

Eco

Stills + 3-second GIFs. Retro buffer off. Perfect for quick bug notes and design callouts.
~2 GB / weekkept 30 d

Standard

720p clips with audio plus a 5-second retro buffer. The sweet spot for agent workflows.
~12 GB / weekkept 60 d

Intensive

1080p clips, 15-second retro buffer, markers. For demos, launch videos, and deep QA sessions.
~40 GB / weekkept 90 d
